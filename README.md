# Images to PDF

Turn any number of pictures into a single PDF — right in your browser, on phone or computer.

**Use it:** https://mfetuah.github.io/images-to-pdf/

- Pick pictures from your gallery, your files, or the camera
- Scroll through them full screen, rotate the ones that are sideways, reorder or remove them
- Pictures you already added are skipped automatically, and the newest/oldest photo dates show where you stopped
- Choose page size (A4, Letter, or fit to picture), margins and quality, then download or share the PDF

Everything runs on your device. Pictures are never uploaded anywhere.

## Run locally

It's a static site with no build step and no dependencies — open `index.html` through any web server
(for example XAMPP: put the folder in `htdocs` and visit `http://localhost/<folder>/`).
